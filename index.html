FurSpace: The Open-Source AI Ecosystem for Pet Care.

We are on a mission to bridge the gap between pet owners, dedicated dog walkers, and veterinary professionals. FurSpace leverages open-source architecture to create a transparent, community-driven platform.

By integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) into our core infrastructure, we are redefining how the pet care community interacts, schedules, and supports one another—building a smarter, safer environment for every furry friend.

Intelligent Tools for Pet People.

Experience the next generation of community apps. FurSpace utilizes advanced AI to ensure seamless connections and reliable information.

AI-Driven Matchmaking

Our models analyze walker schedules, proximity, and pet temperaments to instantly suggest the perfect pairing between dog owners and local walkers.

Automated Vet Advice Parsing

Users can upload physical vet notes. The LLM extracts medication schedules, dietary restrictions, and urgent flags, creating actionable reminders directly in the app.

Smart Community Moderation

The platform automatically analyzes posts and requests to ensure a safe, spam-free environment, flagging inappropriate content before it reaches the feed.
